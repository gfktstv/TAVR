Stay academic.

How to add academicism?

Make your language more scientific by preserving academic words and phrases in your essay and increasing usage by exploring academic word and formulas lists.

What are these lists?

Academic lists are based on words and phrases that occur more often in academic texts than in common writing. Learn more on websites for New Academic Word List (NAWL), Academic Formulas List (AFL), and Academic Collocations List (ACL) you can even find some exercises for practicing AWL!

Avoid cliches.

What is a trigram?

Sequences of 3 words are called trigrams. Some of them tend to be more frequent than others and this may affect how your essay is scored. For instance, phrase "on the one hand" is more popular than "in one way".

Sometimes you should find alternatives for such cliches to make your text more sophisticated. A table with trigrams from the essay will help you find the most frequent ones to be replaced.
